The browser tab now shows live status (#958). Its title gets a prefix — `● ` while a turn is running, `✓ ` on a chat whose reply landed while you were in another tab, `(2) ` for unread replies on a project or Home — and the favicon gets a dot: orange while running, green for unread. A chat open in a background tab is no longer marked read until you actually look at it, so "finished while you were away" now registers in the sidebar and the fleet strip too.
